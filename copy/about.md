## Bio

I'm an interdisciplinary venture-builder who has spent the past decade working at the intersection of technology and low-resource markets across Asia, Africa, and Europe. I co-founded CreditBook, Pakistan's first fintech backed by Tiger Global, where I set up the product and design function from scratch, shaped strategy directly through research, and helped grow annual recurring revenue from $0 to $600K in six months.

My work starts from a simple observation: most systems are designed for the people already inside them. I'm more interested in the ones designed from outside — informal economies, undocumented behavior, the workarounds people build when formal institutions don't serve them. CreditBook grew out of exactly that: a paper ledger practice used by small retailers across Pakistan, rebuilt as free software.

Alongside CreditBook, I spent three years as a Foresight Practitioner at the School of International Futures, running foresight research and training for the World Health Organization, ACCA, the UK Government Office for Science, and Guy's and St Thomas' Hospital — work that sits closer to public policy than product design, and shapes how I think about both.

I hold an MPA in Science, Technology & Public Policy (Distinction) from UCL, an MSc in Innovation & Entrepreneurship from ESADE Business School, and a BSc in Politics, Philosophy and Economics from Warwick. Ultimately, I believe technology should work in harmony with nature.
